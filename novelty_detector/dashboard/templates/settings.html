How Thresholds Work

🔵 Stage 1 — LSH Jaccard
Controls which document pairs enter the semantic stage. Lower → more candidates, more false positives, slower Stage 2. Higher → fewer candidates, may miss near-paraphrases.
🔴 Stage 2 — Semantic Cosine
The cosine similarity threshold for labelling a pair near-duplicate. Typical good values: 0.80 – 0.92.
⛔ Duplicate Jaccard Cutoff
Pairs above this Jaccard score skip Stage 2 entirely and are immediately labelled duplicate. Usually set high (≥ 0.85).
